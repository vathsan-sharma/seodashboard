What We Recommend

  1. Fix the basics. Make every version of the site go to one address, and fix two broken article links. This is a small technical job.
  2. Build pages for what people search. Rewrite the account pages and add MT5 broker and demo account pages.
  3. Measure real results. Connect Search Console and Google Analytics so we can report real visits and sign-ups.

Decisions Needed

  • Approve time to write the account pages and the new MT5 pages.
  • Give access to Google Search Console and Google Analytics.
  • Agree to review the site's links, as some may be paid links.
